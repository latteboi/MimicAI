v0.8.0 Beta — 2026-11-19 (UTC)
The MimicAI Subscription: your characters reply without an API key of your own. Generated characters also each get a voice of their own.
## **Added**
- **The MimicAI Subscription, on the official MimicAI bot: your characters reply without an API key of your own.** Open it with `/subscribe` or `/settings` → API Keys → MimicAI Subscription. Tier 1, 2 or 3 is US$5, 10 or 20 a month plus tax (roughly 800, 2,000 or 5,000 replies), paid through Stripe; checkout and receipts say "Link". Unused replies carry over, up to a month's worth. **Manage** switches tier or cancels, and a cancelled subscription lasts to the end of the month you paid for. 18+. Self-hosted bots don't offer it.
- **The subscription pays for everything your characters do where it's assigned**, not just replies: memories, long-chat summaries, web searches, reading attachments, recall, and a session's critic or AI Director. Assign it to a server like any key and it pays for everyone's chats there. It runs curated models, right now DeepSeek V4.1 Flash & Claude Haiku 5.5, and each profile picks among them on Set Models' **API: Subscription** stop. Not included: images, speech, Adult profiles and age-restricted channels.
- **`/help` → Tickets has a Subscription category.**
- **A generated draft whose message you dismissed by mistake can be reopened.** Press Generate again (`/profile generate`, `/start` or the Public Library) while the draft is still open, and it offers Reopen draft or New character.
- **A generated draft can tick the Instructions boxes before you save it:** Enhance Awareness, Enhanced Roleplay Formatting and Internal Monologue, each with its cost per reply. Nothing is ticked unless you tick it.
- **Every character keeps three limits in every channel, age-restricted ones included:** no sexual content involving anyone under 18 or a real person, no encouraging suicide, self-harm or disordered eating, and no damaging made-up claims about real people presented as fact. When a person, not a character, seems at real risk, the character steps out of the story briefly to point them to help. The bot owner can reword them in `/mod` → Prompts, not remove them.
## **Changed**
- **The Terms of Service and Privacy Policy are revised, effective 19 November 2026.** Terms 11 covers the MimicAI Subscription (who sells it, what it covers, billing, cancelling and refunds) and 12 the governing law. Terms 3 adds the limits every character keeps, what a character does when someone seems at risk, and how anyone, with or without an account, can report a message a character posted, by email or ticket; Terms 4 limits our liability as far as the law allows. The Policy adds the Subscription Record, payments through Stripe, requests the Subscription pays for going through MimicAI's own OpenRouter account, and reports. `/terms` now shows all of it. You do not need to accept them again.
- **`/start` offers Subscription (recommended), OpenRouter or Google** where the subscription is sold. None is no longer offered; if you chose it before, you keep it. `/settings` → API Keys lists the Subscription first.
- **Deleting your account cancels your subscription at once**, and the `/privacy` export includes its tier, status and usage.
- **Generated characters no longer share one voice.** The generator was shown a single finished character as its example, and drafts copied it: short sentences, no emojis, ending on a question, guarded with a soft side. It now sees only the format, and writes each character's way of speaking from who they are.
- **A character that can't speak in a channel is refused when you add it, not announced every round.** The cast editor and `/session swap` say why, such as an Adult 18+ profile outside an age-restricted channel. One already in the cast is skipped quietly and marked ⛔ with the reason in `/session config`, `/session swap` and `/session view`. Call it by name and you're told why, once an hour. A session won't start in an age-restricted channel on a server the MimicAI subscription pays for.
- **`/start` is your home once you're set up.** Run it again and it opens on Home: manage a character, this channel's session, the Library, Settings, your subscription and Help, each a press away. Where something is still left to set up here, such as adding a character to this channel, it shows that step first, with 🏠 Home beside it.
- **`/start` keeps up by itself.** Finish a step in another window — save a generated character, borrow one from the Library, start the cast, get the first reply — and the checklist ticks it and moves on within a few seconds. 🔄 Refresh still re-reads it at once.
