v0.7.3 Beta — 2026-10-15 (UTC)
Everything since v0.7.0, gathering v0.7.1 (2026-10-12), v0.7.2 (2026-10-15) and v0.7.3. Characters remember how they feel about each person, change face with their mood and know the server they are in; profiles come from character cards and pictures and go into the Public Library in one press; and the Terms, the Privacy Policy and the content rating are revised.
## **Upgrading**
- **Run `pip install -r requirements.txt`**: `/start`'s Create Account form needs discord.py 2.7.1 or later.
## **Added**
- **Bonds** (`/profile manage` → Neuro Engine): a second switch beside the hormones. The character keeps Affection and Resentment, 0–100, for each person it talks to, characters included. A first meeting sets both freely; after that each moves 15 at most a reply. Up to 100 people; Reset bonds clears them.
- **See how a character feels about you**: `/session view` → select a participant shows its Bonds with you in words, and `/profile manage` → Neuro Engine shows you your own levels.
- **Mood avatars** (Neuro Engine → Mood avatars…): an avatar each for high joy, stress, affection and excitement, worn while that level is at or above 70, or a level you set.
- **Server Context** (`/session config` → Server Context): characters know the server's name, the channel's name and topic, and the server's custom emojis, which they can use. On by default.
- **Names as Wakewords** (`/session config` → Reactivity): a character's name is a wakeword, so saying it gets an answer whatever its Chance. A character naming another in its reply hands that one the next turn. On by default.
- **Line Spacing** (`/profile manage` → Misc → Line Spacing): removes the blank lines a model pads a reply with, keeping only the gaps you choose. Off by default.
- **Character cards**: `/import` takes a SillyTavern or Chub card, PNG or JSON. Its text becomes a draft to review, its example exchanges training examples, its picture the avatar. A card tagged NSFW is saved as Adult 18+. A card over 4 MB comes in without its picture; set an avatar under Appearance.
- **Lorebooks** (`/profile manage` → Training → Manage Lorebook): up to 50 entries a character is handed when a keyword comes up. A card's lorebook comes with it; `/import` with `profile_name` adds a SillyTavern World Info file to a profile you own.
- **Generate a profile from a picture**: `/profile generate` takes an `image` of the character (PNG, JPEG or WebP, up to 4 MB), with or without a concept, and the Generate form in `/start` and the Public Library takes one too. The draft takes the character's look from it, and the image becomes the avatar.
- **Gemini 4 Argon**, Google's new flagship reasoning model, in Set Models for Google profiles. Paid tier only; thinking effort runs Low, Medium or High.
- **⏯️ Speak Again**: react to give that message's own author one more turn. ▶️ still gives the next participant one.
- **Delete Profile Message** (right-click → Apps): server admins delete a profile's reply, every message of it, from the channel and the session's memory. In a DM with the bot, anyone can delete one of its messages there, without `/purge` taking the newest ones with it.
- **Mimic Dice** (`/play dice`): Liar's Dice for the channel's cast and anyone who sits down. Raise the bid or call Liar from your own cup panel. Each character bids and calls by its temperament, and its bluffs show on its mood: an anxious one starts to look rattled, a steady one keeps a straight face. Choose how many dice each player starts with, and whether ones are wild.
- **Publish in one press**: **Publish Yours** (`/profile hub` → Public Library), **Publish to Library** (`/profile manage` → Sharing), and on a profile `/profile generate` has just saved. A profile not rated yet is rated by the press, which takes a few seconds, and listed if the verdict is General.
- **Sharing tab** (`/profile manage` → Sharing): Publish or Unpublish, Share Profile, Send as Clone and Library Intro in one place. The dashboard shows how many people borrow the profile.
- **Library Sort and Search**: opens on Most Borrowed, and search by creator, display name or intro as well as name.
- **Stats** (`/profile hub` → Stats): the Library's totals and its most borrowed profile, and how many people borrow yours.
- **`/mod` → System Models → Profile Generator**: the bot owner picks the models `/profile generate` drafts with.
## **Changed**
- **Terms of Service and Privacy Policy revised**, effective 15 October 2026 and shown in full in `/terms`. They cover what is stored and where it goes, how long it is kept, what `/privacy` can delete, what your key pays for beyond the reply you see, that Adult profiles are for those 18 and over, and how to reach us. `/privacy` → Request Data Export now includes your voice recordings.
- **`/start` begins with Create Account.** One form asks you to accept the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy and to choose your provider; submitting it is what sets you up. **Read the Terms** beside it opens `/terms`. The provider buttons now only switch an existing account's provider.
- **Refused rating.** Sexual content involving minors or real, identifiable people, which the Terms forbid outright, is now Refused: the profile runs nowhere and cannot be shared, published or used in Global Chat. A second model checks each refusal, and one it does not confirm goes to the operator's **Review Queue** (`/mod` → Profiles) to **Uphold** or **Clear**. Owners can ask for a review once from Content Safety, and a refused profile is rated again only after an edit.
- **A fairer rating.** Villains, historical figures, satire and hard topics written with care are no longer mistaken for refusals, and the most notorious real figures are rated Adult 18+. Characters built around self-harm, hatred, abusing the user or hard drugs are Adult 18+ too; adults drawn young, and war or crime as story, stay General.
- **Fewer gaps in the rating.** Gemini 3.8 Flash steps in on either provider when the usual model is down or gives no answer, and a profile no model rates waits for the operator instead of passing. A profile declared 18+ is checked before it first runs and after each edit. The operator's Exempt rating is gone, so every profile gets the content filters outside age-restricted channels; one that held it is Unrated again.
- **Strikes.** A confirmed or upheld refusal counts against its owner for a year, and three suspend the account until the operator lifts it. `/settings` → About Me shows your count. Operators see everyone's under `/mod` → Blacklist → Strikes, where a lifted block's record now stays for a year, marked ✔️.
- **`/profile hub` opens on the Public Library**, and Home is now Stats, the last tab. Profile Sharing has no mode toggle: it offers borrows only.
- **Publishing is capped at 10 profiles a day.** Publish Yours rates up to 5 at a time and shows each verdict as it lands.
- **Share and Send as Clone list profiles not rated yet** and rate them when sent, rather than leaving them off the list.
- **Final Fallback is gone** (`/profile manage` → Params). For cover when one provider is down, set a Fallback on the other provider in Set Models.
- **Model screens for the Rolling Synopsis and training analysis**, like Set Models: `/session config` → Compaction → **Summariser Model…**, and Training → **Analyse**. Training analysis now keeps its own models on each profile, also under Set Models.
- **Shipped models.** On OpenRouter, Ling 3.0 Flash VL replaces Space Bunny for the critic and memory, and web search, the Rolling Synopsis, training analysis, the attachment describer and `/profile generate` use Claude Haiku 5.5, each then OpenRouter's free router. Auto · Free runs the free router alone. On Google, training analysis uses Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite, then 3.1 Flash-Lite. The Rolling Synopsis drops its third, cross-provider model.
- **Slow commands say what they are doing.** `/profile generate`, `/import` of a character card, `/help ask`, `/memorise`, `/compact` and Compact Now answer at once with a status that shows how long they have been running, which the result then replaces, instead of "MimicAI is thinking...". `/memorise` names each character as it writes their memories.
- **Called characters keep their place** in sequential sessions: one called by a reply, mention, reaction or name speaks from there, and the rest follow round the cast from it, in this round and later ones. Random sessions only move it up.
- **Speech and generated images are named** after the speaker and how their words begin, `Mira_hello-there-traveller.wav`, so a reply to one tells the model what it said.
- **`/purge`** works in a DM with the bot, where anyone can delete its latest messages. `/clear` is gone.
- **`/export` and `/import` work in servers too.** Every reply, the file included, is visible only to you. Export for Self-Hosted files now import into the official bot too.
## **Fixed**
- **Characters can no longer ping @everyone or roles**, whatever a persona or a message tells them to write.
- **A profile edited since its rating could be shared or published on the old one.** Publishing, sharing, cloning and opening Global Chat now settle the rating first, and editing the lorebook, the image prompt or the name asks about the rating as other edits do. A bulk edit applies the same rule without asking.
- **The Compaction tab no longer looks like it cuts the synopsis short.** Its preview stopped at 900 characters without saying so; it now marks where it is cut, and **📜 Read Synopsis** shows the whole text. The synopsis the cast reads was always complete.
- **Compact Now and `/compact` no longer appear stuck.** Progress shows as it goes, the status always resolves, and a very long session stops short of Discord's 15-minute limit; run it again to finish.
