v0.7.9 Beta — 2026-11-11 (UTC)
Characters can come back to what you told them, and their moods settle on their own.
## **Added**
- **Gemini 4 Carbon**, Google's newest reasoning model, in Set Models for Google profiles. Paid tier only; thinking effort runs Low, Medium or High.
- **Nano Banana 2.1**, Google's new image model, in Set Models for Google profiles. Draws at 1K, 2K or 4K, and takes Minimal, Medium or High thinking; Medium is new in the Thinking level picker, for this model alone.
- **Follow-ups**, a switch in `/session config` → Proactivity, off until you turn it on. A character may choose to come back to something someone told it, such as an exam or a trip, after a wait it picks (minutes up to 7 days). Once that time has passed, it raises it when the person next speaks, or by itself once the channel has had a minute's quiet, with Proactivity on or off. Up to three per character and one per person, dropped a week after falling due. Its rules add about 160 tokens to every reply, in the cached part of the prompt, and no extra model call. `/session audit` shows what a turn set or was reminded of, its Overview lists every one still waiting, and `/mod` can reword both prompts.
- **A variety hint** from the Anti-Repetition Critic, in Lexical and Full. A character whose last few replies all ended on a question, or all opened with an *action*, gets one line on its next turn suggesting it vary that, if it suits the character. No extra model call, about 35 tokens on that turn alone, and `/session audit` shows it.
- **Hosts & Tier on a session's model screens.** Critic Model, Director Model and Summariser Model can pin each OpenRouter model to a host and set a Default Tier, as a profile's Models can. Kept with the session.
## **Changed**
- **New profiles draw with Nano Banana 2.1 and speak with Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS** on Google, with 3.8 Flash Lite TTS as the speech fallback.
- **The AI Director, the session synopsis and the critic think at Low with a dynamic budget** until you set their Thinking. The Director and the synopsis thought at Medium, and the critic's budget was 512 tokens.
- **The AI Director has its own models.** Until you choose in Director Model, it runs its own defaults rather than the LTM Summariser's.
- **Claude Haiku 5.5 is the OpenRouter default** for the Anti-Repetition Critic, the AI Director and the LTM Summariser, with the free router behind it, where Ling was. A profile on MimicAI Auto keeps the LTM model its tier names. On Google nothing changes.
- **`/session config` → Proactivity is laid out by feature.** One row each for Proactivity, the AI Director and Follow-ups, its switch first and its settings beside it. The Director's switch moved here from Director Model, and its instructions have a button of their own, apart from Chance & Cooldown.
- **The Anti-Repetition Critic is set per session, not per profile.** It is in `/session config` → Config → Anti-Repetition Critic, off until an administrator picks a mode, and checks every character in the cast unless you untick one, such as a character with a catchphrase. Full mode's model call was always paid with the server's key, so the server now decides whether to make it. The critic settings, prompt, models and Thinking on each profile are gone from `/profile manage`, Override Defaults and MimicAI Auto, and are no longer used.
- **The AI Director runs on two models, not three.** Its Primary and Fallback, as Director Model shows them; the other provider's model it tried after those is gone.
- **A character's mood settles on its own.** With the Neuro Engine on, each level now comes halfway back to its resting level every 6 hours, so a character left furious overnight has mostly calmed down by morning, where before it stayed furious, in every server, until a reply said otherwise. **Edit hormones…** in `/profile manage` → Tools → Neuro-Endocrine Engine now sets those resting levels. A profile whose levels you set there before settles to 50/20/50/20 until you save them there once more.
- **The page number between ◀ and ▶ jumps to a page.** Press it and type the page you want, on every list paged that way: the session cast, Reactivity, the turn order, round and whisper history, the profile pickers, sharing, timezones and `/mod`'s lists. Lists paged from inside their dropdown already had a Jump.
- **`/mod` → Stats opens on an Overview, and everything it lists can be inspected.** The Overview counts sessions configured, started, in memory, proactive and replying, names the servers holding the most, and shows joins in the last week and what is blocked. Pick a server for its owner, server key and every session in it, with each session's owner, cast and last activity, and Block or Suspend Sessions beside them. Pick a user, or a person on a server's page, for their registration, strikes and sessions, with Manage Profiles going straight to their profiles. Servers sort by newest, sessions or members, and **Find by ID** opens a server, a user or a session's channel.
- **Claude models on OpenRouter use the prompt cache.** A character on Claude that speaks again within 5 minutes pays a tenth of the input price for the part of its prompt it already sent; before, Claude was never cached. Writing to the cache costs a quarter more, once. Other models already cached on their own.
- **OpenRouter keeps a conversation on the host that cached its prompt**, from the first reply. Before, it settled on one only after a cache hit.
## **Fixed**
- **`/session config` settings are kept across a restart.** A Proactivity, Rolling Synopsis or Settle Window setting changed after the bot last restarted was lost at the next restart, unless something else about the session changed with it.
