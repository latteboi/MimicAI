v0.7.6 Beta — 2026-11-02 (UTC)
`/refresh` and `/suspend` ask first, and every reply arrives as an embed.
## **Added**
- **`/refresh` and `/suspend` ask before deleting anything**, saying what goes and what is kept, then show their progress and result in the same message: **Conversation cleared** or **Session ended**, with which characters saved a memory of it. A channel with nothing to lose -- no session, or one not yet started with nobody seated -- is not asked.
- **Create from "Did you mean?"**: when `/profile manage` finds no profile by the name you typed, a button creates it as a new, blank profile and opens it, wherever that name is free and you have room for another.
- **Every confirmation has a Cancel button**, and one left unanswered says nothing was changed instead of leaving a button that does nothing.
- **`/trigger` takes a `profile` and a `note`.** `profile` has that cast member answer, and only them, whatever their chance. `note` (administrators only) gives the cast something to answer this round, read as an AI Director's note is.
- **`/purge` takes a `profile` or a `user`**, deleting only that character's or that person's latest turns. Turns already folded into the synopsis are kept. With `whole_channel`, a `user` reaches their messages from before the session too.

## **Changed**
- **Every reply is an embed**: command answers, refusals and errors alike, instead of a mix of plain text and embeds. An expired `/profile generate` draft stays readable, marked Expired beneath it.
- **Session Config → Proactivity and Compaction list their models** as the profile dashboard does: the model in bold, each fallback on a line beneath it.
- **"Did you mean?" and profile autocomplete catch a typo in one word of a longer name, or in a short one**: `sherlok` finds "sherlock holmes", and `mrf` offers "mrc" ahead of "mr. grizz", where they used to suggest nothing.
- **Session Config → Cast puts the Source dropdown above the profiles it lists**, so you pick where to look first.
- **A "Did you mean?" prompt closes once answered**, saying you may dismiss it, instead of leaving its buttons behind.

## **Fixed**
- **Choosing a suggestion in `/train`'s "Did you mean?" did nothing.** It now arms training for the profile chosen.
- **A character removed while a round was running still took its turn**, and showed in `/session swap`'s list as a guest. It now loses its turn, and the list leaves it out of those waiting.
