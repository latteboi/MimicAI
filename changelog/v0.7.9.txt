v0.7.9 Beta — 2026-11-11 (UTC)
Characters can come back to what you told them, and their moods settle on their own.
## **Added**
- **Gemini 4 Carbon**, Google's newest reasoning model, in Set Models for Google profiles. Paid tier only; thinking runs Low, Medium or High.
- **Nano Banana 2.1**, Google's new image model, for Google profiles: 1K, 2K or 4K, with Minimal, Medium (new, this model only) or High thinking.
- **Follow-ups**, a switch in `/session config` → Proactivity, off by default. A character may choose to come back to something you told it, such as an exam or a trip, after a wait it picks (up to 7 days), raising it when you next speak or after a minute's quiet. Up to three per character and one per person, dropped a week after falling due. About 160 cached tokens a reply, no extra model call. `/session audit` shows them, and `/mod` can reword the prompts.
- **A variety hint** from the Anti-Repetition Critic, in Lexical and Full: a character whose last few replies all ended on a question, or all opened with an *action*, is nudged to vary it. No extra call, about 35 tokens on that turn.
- **Hosts & Tier on a session's model screens**: Critic, Director and Summariser Model can pin each OpenRouter model to a host and set a Default Tier.
## **Changed**
- **New profiles draw with Nano Banana 2.1 and speak with Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS** on Google, with 3.8 Flash Lite TTS as the fallback.
- **The AI Director, the session synopsis and the critic think at Low, dynamic budget** until you set it, instead of Medium (Director, synopsis) and a 512-token budget (critic).
- **The AI Director has its own models**, two of them (Primary and Fallback), instead of the LTM Summariser's.
- **Claude Haiku 5.5 is the OpenRouter default** for the critic, the AI Director and the LTM Summariser, with the free router behind it, where Ling was. MimicAI Auto keeps its tier's LTM model; Google is unchanged.
- **`/session config` → Proactivity is laid out by feature**: one row each for Proactivity, the AI Director and Follow-ups, switch first. The Director's switch and instructions are here now.
- **The Anti-Repetition Critic is set per session**, in `/session config` → Config, off until an administrator picks a mode, for the whole cast unless you untick someone. Full mode was always paid with the server's key, so the server decides. Its per-profile settings are gone.
- **A character's mood settles on its own.** With the Neuro Engine on, each level comes halfway back to rest every 6 hours, so a character left furious overnight has mostly calmed by morning. **Edit hormones…** now sets those resting levels; a profile set there before settles to 50/20/50/20 until saved again.
- **The page number between ◀ and ▶ jumps to a page** on every list paged that way.
- **`/mod` → Stats opens on an Overview** of sessions, busiest servers, recent joins and blocks. A server's page shows its owner, key and sessions, with Block or Suspend Sessions; a user's, their registration, strikes, sessions and profiles. **Find by ID** opens a server, user or session.
- **Claude models on OpenRouter use the prompt cache**: a character speaking again within 5 minutes pays a tenth of the input price for what it already sent, and a quarter more once to write it.
- **OpenRouter keeps a conversation on the host that cached its prompt** from the first reply, not after the first hit.
## **Fixed**
- **`/session config` settings survive a restart.** A Proactivity, Rolling Synopsis or Settle Window change was lost at the next restart unless something else changed with it.
