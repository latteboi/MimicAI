v0.7.3 Beta — 2026-10-15 (UTC)
Revised Terms and Privacy Policy, a content rating with a review step, and model screens for the synopsis and training analysis.
## **Changed**
- **Terms of Service and Privacy Policy revised**, effective 15 October 2026 and shown in full in `/terms`. They cover what is stored and where it goes, how long it is kept, what `/privacy` can delete, what your key pays for beyond the reply you see, that Adult profiles are for those 18 and over, and how to reach us. `/privacy` → Request Data Export now includes your voice recordings.
- **Refused rating.** Sexual content involving minors or real, identifiable people, which the Terms forbid outright, is now Refused: the profile runs nowhere and cannot be shared, published or used in Global Chat. A second model checks each refusal, and one it does not confirm goes to the operator's **Review Queue** (`/mod` → Profiles) to **Uphold** or **Clear**. Owners can ask for a review once from Content Safety, and a refused profile is rated again only after an edit.
- **A fairer rating.** Villains, historical figures, satire and hard topics written with care are no longer mistaken for refusals, and the most notorious real figures are rated Adult 18+. Characters built around self-harm, hatred, abusing the user or hard drugs are Adult 18+ too; adults drawn young, and war or crime as story, stay General.
- **Fewer gaps in the rating.** Gemini 3.8 Flash steps in on either provider when the usual model is down or gives no answer, and a profile no model rates waits for the operator instead of passing. A profile declared 18+ is checked before it first runs and after each edit. The operator's Exempt rating is gone, so every profile gets the content filters outside age-restricted channels; one that held it is Unrated again.
- **Strikes.** A confirmed or upheld refusal counts against its owner for a year, and three suspend the account until the operator lifts it. `/settings` → About Me shows your count. Operators see everyone's under `/mod` → Blacklist → Strikes, where a lifted block's record now stays for a year, marked ✔️.
- **Model screens for the Rolling Synopsis and training analysis**, like Set Models: `/session config` → Compaction → **Summariser Model…**, and Training → **Analyse**. Training analysis now keeps its own models on each profile, also under Set Models.
- **`/start` begins with Create Account.** One form asks you to accept the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy and to choose your provider; submitting it is what sets you up. **Read the Terms** beside it opens `/terms`. The provider buttons now only switch an existing account's provider.
- **Slow commands show how long they have been running**, as a whisper's reply does: `/memorise`, `/compact` and Compact Now, `/help ask`, `/profile generate` and card import. `/memorise` names each character as it writes their memories, and the result takes the status's place in the same box.
- **New shipped models.** On OpenRouter, web search, the Rolling Synopsis, training analysis, the attachment describer and `/profile generate` use Claude Haiku 5.5, then OpenRouter's free router. On Google, training analysis uses Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite, then 3.1 Flash-Lite. The Rolling Synopsis drops its third, cross-provider model.

## **Fixed**
- **The Compaction tab no longer looks like it cuts the synopsis short.** Its preview stopped at 900 characters without saying so; it now marks where it is cut, and **📜 Read Synopsis** shows the whole text. The synopsis the cast reads was always complete.
- **`/help ask` no longer hangs on a long answer.** An answer over 2,000 characters used to leave "Looking that up in the guide..." on screen for good.
- **Compact Now and `/compact` no longer appear stuck.** Progress shows as it goes, the status always resolves, and a very long session stops short of Discord's 15-minute limit; run it again to finish.
