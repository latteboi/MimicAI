v0.7.8 Beta — 2026-11-10 (UTC)
`/invite` says what MimicAI is and what to do next.
## **Added**
- **Enhanced Roleplay Formatting and Internal Monologue**, two boxes under Enhance Awareness in `/profile manage` → Instructions. Formatting writes each reply as beats: a line of unquoted dialogue, then a small italic action line under it. Monologue ends every reply with one small line of the character's private thought. Either works alone, both are off until ticked, and like Enhance Awareness the text is kept up to date for you rather than copied into the profile.
- **MimicAI's Guidance** in `/profile manage` → Persona shows the exact text each of those boxes adds, and which are on for that profile.
## **Changed**
- **Characters no longer talk as if they were reading a screen.** The rules every session sends no longer call the conversation a Discord chat. A character is wherever its persona and the scene put it, and face to face with everyone when neither says. It also reads the name on each turn as the speaker, where it could mistake it for bookkeeping and greet someone from its own backstory instead.
- **`/invite` rewritten.** It says what MimicAI is, what it costs and to run `/start` once it is added, and lists exactly the permissions the invite asks for. If you own child bots, it also gives you each one's invite link, privately: until now that link was shown only once, when the bot was linked.
- **A self-hosted bot's invite link no longer asks for Administrator.** Every instance now asks for only the permissions the bot uses, as the official bot always has. A server you added your bot to before keeps whatever it granted then.
- **`/start` says where to get a missing child bot's link** when one is not in the server.
- **`/profile manage` settings are named, not instructed.** "Set Models", "Configure Anti-Repetition Critic", "Manage Lorebook" and the like are now "Models", "Anti-Repetition Critic" and "Lorebook", as Response Mode and Line Spacing always were. Rows that do something to the profile — Rename, Duplicate, Delete, Share — keep their verb.
- **The session synopsis keeps the facts a story needs.** It now ends in a list of Standing facts — debts, promises, secrets, true names, who holds what — that each fold carries forward as written, instead of retelling them until they drop out. Its default length is 300 words, up from 220, to make room; a session that set its own length keeps it.
- **Profile menus open faster.** Autocomplete, the Share and Publish menus and the Public Library now read each profile's name and rating from one small encrypted index, instead of opening every profile to find them. Searching the Library no longer opens every listing either.
- **Enhance Awareness is one prompt.** Its group half, sent only with two or more characters seated, is folded into the one text and sent the same alone or in a group. One character on its own now also gets the guidance on turn times and on not looping. It costs about 300 tokens more alone, and less in a group.
- **Memories keep who did what.** A long-term memory now says who acted and who started it, quotes only words actually said, and keeps a running joke, grudge or prank that landed instead of skipping all banter. A profile with its own memory prompt keeps it.
## **Fixed**
- **A form closed without saving can be opened again straight away.** Picking the same dropdown row twice did nothing until another row was picked first, in `/profile manage`, the bulk editor and Override Defaults.
- **Every reply waited up to a second after the model had finished.** A reply now posts as soon as it lands, so a round with two characters finishes about a second sooner.
- **A session synopsis cut off mid-sentence was kept.** It replaced the whole one before it, and the characters lost whatever it had not reached. A synopsis that is cut off, or that drops its Standing facts, is now refused: the next model tries, and failing that the turns wait to be folded later.
