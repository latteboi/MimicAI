v0.8.4 Beta — 2026-11-25 (UTC)
You can erase every memory a character wrote from a conversation you were in, and characters know you by an ID nobody can trace back to your Discord account.
## **Added**
- **Erase Memories of Me, in `/data`.** Erases every memory any character wrote from a conversation you spoke in or were quoted in, whoever owns the character. A memory written from the same conversation goes too, even one about someone else. It works without an account. Memories written before this version don't record who spoke, so it can't find them.
## **Changed**
- **Characters know you by a new ID**, made with a key only this bot holds, so nobody can work out your Discord account from it. `/settings` shows it. Bonds carry over to the new ID.
