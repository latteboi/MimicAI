v0.8.0 Beta — 2026-11-19 (UTC)
The MimicAI Subscription: your characters reply without an API key of your own. Generated characters also each get a voice of their own.
## **Added**
- **The MimicAI Subscription, on the official MimicAI bot: your characters reply without an API key of your own.** Open it with `/subscribe` or `/settings` → API Keys → MimicAI Subscription. Tier 1, 2 or 3 is US$5, 10 or 20 a month plus tax (roughly 5,200, 13,500 or 33,000 replies), paid through Stripe (checkout says "Link"). Unused replies carry over, up to a month's worth. **Manage** switches tier or cancels, and a cancelled one lasts to the end of the month you paid for. 18+. Self-hosted bots don't offer it.
- **It pays for everything your characters do where it's assigned**: replies, memories, summaries, web searches, attachments, recall, and a session's critic or AI Director. Assigned to a server like any key, it pays for everyone's chats there. It runs curated models, right now DeepSeek V4.1 Flash and Claude Haiku 5.5, picked per profile on Set Models' **API: Subscription**. Not included: images, speech, Adult profiles and age-restricted channels.
- **`/help` → Tickets has a Subscription category.**
- **A dismissed generated draft can be reopened.** Press Generate again (`/profile generate`, `/start` or the Public Library) while it's open: Reopen draft or New character.
- **A generated draft can tick the Instructions boxes before you save it:** Enhance Awareness, Enhanced Roleplay Formatting and Internal Monologue, each with its cost per reply.
- **Every character keeps three limits in every channel, age-restricted ones included:** no sexual content involving anyone under 18 or a real person, no encouraging suicide, self-harm or disordered eating, and no damaging made-up claims about real people as fact. If a person, not a character, seems at real risk, the character briefly steps out of the story to point them to help. The bot owner can reword the limits in `/mod` → Prompts, not remove them.
## **Changed**
- **The Terms of Service and Privacy Policy are revised, effective 19 November 2026.** Terms 11 covers the Subscription and 12 the governing law. Terms 3 adds the limits above, what a character does when someone seems at risk, and how anyone, account or not, can report a character's message; Terms 4 limits our liability as far as the law allows. The Policy adds the Subscription Record, Stripe payments, requests paid for through MimicAI's OpenRouter account, and reports. `/terms` shows all of it. No need to accept again.
- **`/start` offers Subscription (recommended), OpenRouter or Google** where the subscription is sold. None is no longer offered; if you chose it before, you keep it.
- **Deleting your account cancels your subscription at once**, and the `/privacy` export includes its tier, status and usage.
- **Generated characters no longer share one voice.** The generator was shown one finished character as its example, and drafts copied it: short sentences, no emojis, ending on a question, a guarded manner with a soft side. It now sees only the format and writes each way of speaking from who the character is.
- **A character that can't speak in a channel is refused when you add it, not announced every round.** The cast editor and `/session swap` say why (an Adult 18+ profile outside an age-restricted channel, say). One already in the cast is skipped quietly and marked ⛔ with the reason in `/session config`, `swap` and `view`; call it by name and you're told why, once an hour. A session won't start in an age-restricted channel on a server the subscription pays for.
- **`/start` is your home once you're set up.** It opens on Home: a character, this channel's session, the Library, Settings, your subscription and Help, each a press away. Anything left to set up here shows first, with 🏠 Home beside it.
- **`/start` keeps up by itself.** Finish a step in another window and the checklist ticks it within a few seconds. 🔄 Refresh re-reads it at once.
